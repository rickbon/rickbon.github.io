Publications & Manuscripts

A Problem-Oriented Survey of Anomalous Sound Detection: From Normality Learning to Deployable Acoustic Intelligence
Fengtao He et al. · Computer Speech & Language Conditional Accept

A systematic review of anomalous sound detection from normality learning to domain generalization, few-shot learning, and real-world deployment, with emphasis on data scarcity, domain shift, score stability, and deployability.

Passive Underwater Acoustic Target Recognition: A Task- and Protocol-Aware Review
Second Author · Computer Speech & Language Under Review

A review of passive underwater acoustic target recognition that analyzes task settings, dataset protocols, acoustic representations, and the transition from closed-set recognition to open-set, cross-domain, few-shot, and continual learning scenarios.

Combining Robotic Vision with Non-Invasive Brain-Computer Interfaces: Development and Future Directions
First Author · Highlights in Science, Engineering and Technology Accepted

A study of integrating non-invasive brain-computer interfaces with robotic vision, including EEG noise, decoding efficiency, visual localization, control accuracy, and multimodal challenges.
